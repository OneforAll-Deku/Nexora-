## 2. Core Value Propositions & Talking Points

Your video must convey these **4 pillars** clearly:

1. **Autonomous Multimodal Vision**:
   - Legacy OCR uses rigid bounding-box templates that shatter whenever an invoice font changes.
   - Nexora uses Google Gemini 2.5 Flash to understand messy PDF invoices, skewed smartphone photos, multi-currency receipts, and complex line-item tables with zero prior template training.

2. **3-Tier Fraud & Error Shield**:
   - **Arithmetic Sanity Check**: Calculates `(Quantity × Unit Price) + Tax == Total` to the penny, catching supplier calculation errors.
   - **Duplicate Detection**: Cross-references vendor name and invoice number against the general ledger to prevent double payouts.
   - **Statistical $Z$-Score Surge Protection ($Z \ge 2.5\sigma$)**: Tracks historical pricing per SKU to stop erroneous or inflated price surges before wire approval.

3. **Split-Screen Reconciliation Canvas**:
   - Side-by-side interactive viewer: live invoice document on the left (zoom, pan, invert) + audited ledger form on the right. Instant one-click commit to master ledger.

4. **Zero-Cost BYOK Architecture**:
   - Free forever hosting on Vercel + Cloudflare.
   - Bring your own Gemini API key (1,500 free daily requests on Google AI Studio) or OpenRouter key. All keys encrypted locally at rest with AES-Fernet cryptography.
